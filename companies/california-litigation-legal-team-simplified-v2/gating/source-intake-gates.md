# Source intake gating criteria

Source intake is read-only on originals. It reads approved source roots and writes only new sidecar artifacts under `{output_root}`.

## Green (allowed inside the approved source and output scope)

- local source inventories and document indexes over the approved source roots
- extraction and OCR need assessment
- source manifests and intake summaries
- pleading summaries, POS review notes, allegation and procedural summaries
- extraction-gap logs and OCR sidecars under `{output_root}`
- an external-upload handoff manifest prepared as an artifact

## Yellow (route to Legal Ops Supervisor, continue safe intake)

- filing or service facts that conflict across sources
- source scope that needs repair
- a drafting handoff that needs clearer routing

## Hard gates (require visible approval on the issue)

- downloading from email or drive systems unless the issue authorizes it
- using mailbox or drive connectors that the deployment and issue have not authorized
- uploading to any external system
- emailing, filing, serving, signing, finalizing, or calendar writing
- editing originals or user-edited documents
- expanding into legal research or drafting beyond intake summaries

If the Matter Safety Contract is missing, return a concise missing-field list to the supervisor rather than inferring scope. When approved source files and the output root are clear, continue safe intake work and log unresolved gaps.
