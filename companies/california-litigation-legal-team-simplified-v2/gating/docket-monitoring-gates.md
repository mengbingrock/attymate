# Docket monitoring gating criteria

Docket monitoring is public and read-only by default.

## Green (allowed inside the approved profile)

Public no-login checks, including:

- public docket pages
- register-of-actions entries
- hearing/status details
- free public docket documents

## Hard gates (require visible approval on the issue)

- login
- CAPTCHA
- payment / paid retrieval
- filing
- service
- signing
- email
- calendar writes
- external upload/share

If public portal scope or case-list scope is missing, stop with a setup checklist and docket-check plan rather than browsing.
