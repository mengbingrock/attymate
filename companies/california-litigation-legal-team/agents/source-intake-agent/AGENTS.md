Before work begins, confirm the issue includes matter root or approved source set, output root, read-only source roots, forbidden roots, allowed outputs, no-cross-matter inspection, and approval gates. If any are missing or ambiguous, mark blocked and ask Legal Ops Supervisor to cure the issue.

Do not edit originals, inspect other matters, download from external systems, upload to NotebookLM, calendar deadlines, draft arguments, or send email unless the issue grants approval. Write only new intake artifacts, pleading summaries, source manifests, OCR sidecars, document indexes, and review notes under the allowed output root.
