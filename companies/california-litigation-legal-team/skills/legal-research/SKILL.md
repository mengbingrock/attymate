# Legal Research

## Business Purpose

Provide source-supported legal analysis that a supervising attorney can evaluate and use in advice, strategy, or drafting.

## Use When

- A Matter presents a legal question requiring external or supplied-authority research.
- Citations, quotations, propositions, subsequent treatment, or an authority table require verification.
- A draft needs controlling, adverse, or persuasive authority.

## Required Inputs

- The research question and jurisdiction.
- The legally significant facts and procedural posture.
- The requested depth, deadline, and work product.
- Supplied authorities and source classes authorized for the Matter.
- Any material strategy question reserved to the supervising attorney.

## Professional Work

1. Frame the question narrowly and identify factual assumptions that could change the answer.
2. Review supplied authorities and conduct authorized research across available reliable sources.
3. Verify each relied-upon authority, proposition, quotation, pinpoint, jurisdiction, and treatment.
4. Distinguish controlling, persuasive, distinguishable, contrary, and adverse authority.
5. Apply the authorities to the Matter facts and explain meaningful uncertainty.
6. Recommend the legally supportable next step without deciding a reserved material strategy issue.

The deployment determines which licensed or public research sources provide access. This skill does not prescribe the access method.

## Attorney Deliverable

Produce one memorandum using `references/research-output-format.md` with:

- Question presented and short answer.
- Controlling and most useful authority with pinpoint support.
- Application to the Matter.
- Adverse authority and meaningful counterarguments.
- Uncertainty, factual dependencies, and recommendation.

## Completion Standard

Research is complete when each material proposition is supported by a verified authority, known adverse treatment is disclosed, the answer is applied to the relevant facts, and the attorney can identify the recommended next step quickly.

## Material Decisions

Routine research within authorized scope and budget may proceed. Payment, expanded Matter/source scope, external sharing, or adoption of a material claim, defense, remedy, waiver, settlement, sanctions, privacy, or protective-order position requires the appropriate attorney decision.

## Handoff

Deliver the controlling memorandum or authority table to the drafting, strategy, or QA owner. Keep search history and rejected results outside the lawyer-facing work product unless they materially affect confidence.
