# California Motion Practice

## Business Purpose

Produce a coherent California motion package that is procedurally appropriate, supported by verified facts and authority, and ready for supervising-attorney review.

## Use When

- The Matter requires a motion, opposition, reply, memorandum, declaration, proposed order, separate statement, request for judicial notice, or related package.
- Motion-related strategy or procedural readiness requires analysis before drafting.

This capability covers motion practice generally. A subpoena motion to compel is one supported profile, not a separate intake system.

## Required Inputs

- Motion type, procedural posture, objective, requested relief, and known deadlines.
- Verified facts, source documents, authorities, prior orders, and relevant correspondence.
- Required package components and applicable court or judge requirements.
- Material positions already decided or reserved to the attorney.

## Professional Work

1. Confirm the procedural vehicle, legal standard, requested relief, and filing/service context.
2. Identify the elements, defenses, evidentiary support, adverse facts, and controlling or adverse authority.
3. Determine which package components are required for the particular motion.
4. Draft each component from verified sources with consistent facts, defined terms, citations, and requested relief.
5. Address meaningful counterarguments and disclose unsupported or uncertain propositions.
6. Review the complete package for procedural fit, source support, exhibit references, numbering, placeholders, and consistency.

Use `references/subpoena-mtc-checklists.md` only when that motion profile applies.

## Attorney Deliverable

Provide one controlling motion package with:

- Reviewable drafts of the required components.
- The principal theory and requested relief.
- Important factual, authority, or procedural risks.
- Material decisions with recommendations.
- Package readiness and the next action.

## Completion Standard

Motion work is complete when the package is internally consistent, procedurally responsive, supported by verified facts and authorities, candid about material weaknesses, and ready for attorney review.

## Material Decisions

The supervising attorney decides whether to pursue the motion, select or change material relief, waive rights, seek sanctions, or adopt another significant litigation position. Filing, service, signature, sending, paid retrieval, external sharing, expanded scope, and protected-file changes require the applicable decision.

## Handoff

Send the controlling package to QA or the supervising attorney. Link to supporting research and source material rather than repeating it in status messages.
