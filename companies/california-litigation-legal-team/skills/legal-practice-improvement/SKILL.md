# Legal Practice Improvement

## Business Purpose

Improve the firm's legal service and internal standards by turning authorized experience into reusable, non-client-specific practice guidance.

## Use When

- The firm expressly authorizes review of completed or ongoing work for improvement purposes.
- Legal Ops requests a private Firm Operations Guide proposal.
- The firm requests a sanitized proposal for a reusable public legal capability.

Do not use this capability without explicit learning authority, allowed sources, and exclusions.

## Required Inputs

- Learning mode: off, private firm improvement, or sanitized public proposal.
- The authorized work and sources that may be reviewed.
- Material that must not be learned or repeated.
- Intended audience and destination for the proposal.
- The person authorized to approve any resulting change.

## Professional Work

1. Review only the expressly authorized records and work products.
2. Identify recurring legal-practice lessons, quality controls, decision points, and communication improvements.
3. Separate firm-specific preferences from broadly reusable legal guidance.
4. Exclude client facts, privileged strategy, confidential source text, credentials, private locations, and Matter identifiers.
5. Explain the evidence and confidence supporting each proposed improvement.
6. Propose changes for review; do not alter live instructions or public material without approval.

## Attorney Deliverable

Provide one concise improvement proposal stating:

- Sources reviewed and limits.
- Reusable legal-practice lessons.
- Private firm proposals.
- Sanitized public proposals, if authorized.
- Excluded material and unresolved risk.
- Recommended approver and next action.

## Completion Standard

The proposal is complete when each recommendation is traceable to authorized experience, useful beyond a single Matter, stripped of protected information, and presented for the correct level of review.

## Material Decisions

Approval is required before changing the Firm Operations Guide, live agent instructions, public package material, or the authorized learning scope. Observation and proposal drafting may proceed within the existing learning authority.

## Handoff

Send private practice proposals to Legal Ops and sanitized public proposals to the package owner. Never place protected Matter content in a reusable skill.
