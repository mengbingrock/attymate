# Legal Document Readiness Standard

Apply this standard to productions, correspondence, declarations, pleadings, exhibits, transcripts, administrative records, and other approved legal documents.

- Preserve each original document's identity, content, page order, and evidentiary role.
- Account for every authorized source file and every page.
- Keep the original page as the final authority when derived text conflicts with the image.
- Provide a stable source and page reference for every page represented in the review set.
- Identify blank, unreadable, missing, duplicated, rotated, handwritten, redacted, or uncertain pages.
- Do not impose exhibit numbers or legal characterizations by renaming source files.
- For large document sets, preserve completed review and coverage information so work does not need to be repeated.
- Report only exceptions that affect legal completeness, confidence, timing, or the next action.

A document set is ready for downstream legal work when every authorized page is reviewable or expressly identified as a material exception.
