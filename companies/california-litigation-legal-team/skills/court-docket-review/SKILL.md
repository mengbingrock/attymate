# Court Docket Review

## Business Purpose

Tell the legal team what materially changed on a court docket, why it matters, and what action should follow.

## Use When

- The team needs a current procedural status for an authorized case.
- A filing, order, hearing, service event, or register entry may affect strategy or deadlines.
- An approved docket monitor reports a possible change.

## Required Inputs

- Court, jurisdiction, case number, and authorized case scope.
- Relevant time period or last confirmed docket event.
- The legal or procedural question to answer.
- Any known deadline policy or related Matter event.

## Professional Work

1. Confirm the case identity and scope before reviewing docket information.
2. Identify new or materially changed entries and distinguish them from duplicates or non-substantive updates.
3. Record the reliable source and date for each material event.
4. Separate confirmed docket facts from legal inference and access limitations.
5. Identify any procedural, filing, service, hearing, or deadline consequence.
6. Send potential deadline consequences to Litigation Deadline Management with the supporting trigger source.

The deployment determines how authorized court information is accessed. No particular court portal or access method is part of this skill.

## Attorney Deliverable

Use `references/docket-output-format.md` to state:

- The material docket event.
- The reliable source and date.
- Its practical legal or procedural effect.
- Any reliability limitation.
- The recommended action and owner.

## Completion Standard

Review is complete when the material change is verified, its effect is explained, and any resulting deadline or action has a clear owner. A no-change review ends without a lawyer-facing report.

## Material Decisions

Paid retrieval, expanded case/court scope, filing, service, sending, calendar writing, or external sharing requires the applicable attorney decision.

## Handoff

Send verified dates to Litigation Deadline Management and substantive events to the relevant legal workstream. Keep access history and duplicate checks in operational records.
