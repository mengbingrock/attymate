# Subpoena MTC Checklists

Use these checks before presenting any subpoena MTC package as review-ready.

Source checks:

- Subpoena, proof/service facts, responses, objections, meet-and-confer records, productions, declarations, and relevant pleadings are inventoried.
- Original request and objection text is used for separate statement items.
- Evidence Code section 1561 or custodian-affidavit issues are identified when business-records production defects matter.
- Privacy, protective order, burden, relevance, and sanctions issues are routed as strategy gates when not already approved.

Draft checks:

- Requested relief matches the subpoena categories and meet-and-confer history.
- Factual assertions cite approved sources.
- Legal propositions cite supplied or approved authorities.
- TOA includes only authorities actually used.
- Exhibit, RJN, declaration, and separate-statement numbering is consistent.
- Stale shell names, banks, dates, case numbers, hearing details, and placeholders are removed or flagged.
- No finalization, filing, service, signature, email, or active Word overwrite occurred without approval.
