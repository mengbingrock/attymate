# lexis-browseros-legal-research

## Use

Load for external or supplied-authority research, Lexis/browser research, citation verification, Shepardizing/treatment review, authority tables, or a legal research memorandum.

## Required Handoff

Use the parent Matter Authorization Package and a focused research work order stating the question, jurisdiction, relevant facts, source classes/tools, budget, output, and any material strategy reserved to the lawyer.

## Procedure

1. Frame a narrow question and identify factual predicates.
2. Review supplied authorities and conduct routine authorized external research when needed; do not ask per query or per authority.
3. Verify every authority against a reliable source. Record treatment, jurisdiction, proposition, and pinpoint support.
4. Keep queries, filters, rejected results, and tool activity in the internal research log.
5. Produce one attorney-facing research document using `references/research-output-format.md`.
6. Identify adverse authority, uncertainty, and any material litigation choice separately from the analysis.

## Authorization And Interruptions

Configured authenticated research tools, new verified authorities, and permitted downloads proceed within parent scope and budget. Login expiry, MFA, or connector failure routes to Legal Ops/tool owner as an operational interruption. Payment, budget/scope expansion, external sharing, and adoption of material strategy require the appropriate attorney decision.

## Communication

Lead with the short answer and application. Do not expose search process, tool names, query logs, or approval jargon. Comment only to link a reviewable result, material change, decision, attorney-owned blocker, or completion.

## Limits

- Never cite authority from memory.
- Never omit known adverse treatment.
- Never research another matter or exceed authorized scope/budget.
- Never decide a material claim/defense/relief/waiver/settlement/sanctions/privacy position for the lawyer.
