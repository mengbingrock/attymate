# Word Editing Protocol

Designated working-copy creation and revision proceed under the parent Matter Authorization Package.

Safe defaults:

- Confirm the source draft, designated working copy, output path, requested action, and rollback strategy before editing.
- For Motion Package work, confirm the motion profile, controlling issue/fact/authority tables, captions, exhibit numbering, supporting papers, TOC/TOA status, and placeholders.
- Draft text in artifacts or comments before active-draft insertion when that reduces risk.
- Create new working copies rather than modifying originals.
- Preserve captions, styles, numbering, indentation, fields, headers, footers, tables, and paragraph spacing.
- Do not accept/reject tracked changes unless expressly approved.
- Do not remove comments unless expressly approved.
- Do not overwrite user-edited, final, signed, filed, served, or gold-standard documents.
- Record every approved active-draft update in the internal change log.

After editing, render or inspect the document enough to verify formatting did not materially break. In the lawyer-facing handoff, identify only material revisions, unresolved legal issues, and the review link.
