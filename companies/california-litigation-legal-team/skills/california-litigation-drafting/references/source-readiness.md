# Source Readiness For Drafting

Use this standard when drafting depends on pleadings, evidence, correspondence, orders, transcripts, prior drafts, or other Matter documents.

- Confirm that every relied-upon source is within the authorized Matter scope.
- Preserve source documents as received and use stable source/page references.
- Determine whether the available review set is complete enough for the requested legal product.
- Record unreadable pages, missing attachments, uncertain text, inconsistent versions, and unresolved provenance.
- Preserve pleading paragraph numbers and other source locators when they will support drafting or review.
- Do not draft around a material source gap. State the gap, its effect, and the best next action.

Legal Document Intake owns document usability and page coverage. Drafting owns the legal significance of any remaining source limitation.
