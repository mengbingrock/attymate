# Intermediary Work Product

Build intermediary artifacts when they reduce drafting risk or make QA easier.

Use only the intermediary artifacts that reduce drafting risk or make QA easier:

- Source manifest/index: file, source root, date, type, page count if known, extraction status, use restrictions, and relevance.
- Chronology table: date, event, source citation, issue relevance, confidence, and procedural significance.
- Issue/fact table: issue or element, supporting fact, missing fact, source cite, counterpoint, draft treatment, and owner.
- Authority table: proposition, authority, pinpoint, treatment status, source, and approval status.
- Exhibit or RJN list: exhibit, source file, description, purpose, sponsor/declarant, and citation target.
- Replacement table: old shell term, replacement term, source support, and unresolved placeholders.
- Needed-input or decision list: question, recommended default, alternatives, legal effect/deadline, gate type, and affected artifact.
- Change log: date/time, artifact, change, source basis, reviewer instruction, and unresolved risks.

For Motion Package work, add a live outline of required sections and supporting papers. For a subpoena MTC, add subpoena-category and objection/response tables from `motion-practice/subpoena-mtc-checklists.md`; do not create them for unrelated work.

Keep intermediary artifacts separate from final court-facing text. Do not turn assumptions into factual assertions.
