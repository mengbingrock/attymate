# Subpoena MTC Checklist

Use only for a subpoena motion to compel.

- Inventory subpoena, proof/service facts, responses, objections, meet-and-confer records, productions, declarations, and relevant pleadings.
- Use original request and objection text for separate-statement items.
- Identify Evidence Code section 1561 or custodian-affidavit issues when business-records defects matter.
- Route unresolved privacy, protective-order, burden, relevance, and sanctions issues as strategy gates.
- Confirm requested relief matches subpoena categories and meet-and-confer history; verify exhibit, RJN, declaration, separate-statement, and TOA numbering.
- Do not finalize, file, serve, sign, send, or overwrite protected material without the required attorney decision.
