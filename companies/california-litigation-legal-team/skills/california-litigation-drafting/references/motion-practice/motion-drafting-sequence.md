# Motion Package Drafting Sequence

Use this sequence only for the Motion Package profile. Load `subpoena-mtc-checklists.md` only for a subpoena MTC.

1. Confirm the motion profile, procedural posture, objective, requested relief, deadlines, sources, output, and recorded material decisions.
2. Review only the relevant intake, fact, research, deadline, and draft artifacts.
3. Build the minimum source, issue/fact, exhibit/RJN, authority, chronology, and replacement artifacts needed.
4. Draft the factual and procedural narrative from source citations, including material adverse facts.
5. Draft and assemble required components, such as memorandum, declaration, exhibits/RJN, proposed order, separate statement, TOC, or TOA.
6. Run QA for procedural fit, source support, requested relief, package consistency, numbering, placeholders, and protected/final boundaries.
7. Present one controlling review package identifying material revisions, unresolved legal issues, and any attorney decision.

Do not invent authority, procedural facts, evidentiary support, requested relief, sanctions basis, privacy facts, protective-order terms, or compliance history.
