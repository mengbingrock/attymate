# California Pleading Review

## Business Purpose

Give the legal team a reliable, source-linked understanding of a California pleading and its practical consequences.

## Use When

- A complaint, answer, cross-complaint, amended pleading, demurrer-related pleading, or similar filing enters a Matter.
- The team needs pleading facts before research, drafting, calendaring, or strategy review.

## Required Inputs

- The pleading set authorized for review.
- The Matter and procedural objective.
- Known filing, service, hearing, or response information.
- Any question the supervising attorney wants answered first.

## Professional Work

1. Confirm the pleading identity, version, court, case number, parties, and apparent filing/service status.
2. Determine whether every page is readable enough for legal reliance; use Legal Document Intake when it is not.
3. Identify material allegations, admissions, denials, claims, defenses, requested relief, attached exhibits, and incorporated material.
4. Review signature, verification, caption, party, and procedural features relevant to the requested objective.
5. Separate confirmed content from ambiguity, omission, inference, and unreadable material.
6. Link each material conclusion to a stable page or source reference.

## Attorney Deliverable

Produce one pleading review using `references/intake-output-format.md` that states:

- What was reviewed and whether the set appears complete.
- The short procedural and substantive bottom line.
- Material allegations, responses, relief, and filing/service facts.
- Reliability limits or missing material that could change the analysis.
- Recommended next legal action or workstream.

## Completion Standard

The review is complete when all authorized pleadings are accounted for, material conclusions are traceable to the source, and any exception affecting legal reliance is explicit.

## Material Decisions

The skill may identify potential claims, defenses, responses, and risks. The supervising attorney decides whether to adopt or abandon a material legal position, expand the source scope, or take an external act.

## Handoff

Provide downstream facts, deadlines, and legal questions directly to the responsible capability. Do not send extraction details or document inventories to the lawyer unless they affect completeness or reliability.
