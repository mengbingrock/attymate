# Firm Calendar Policy Template

Deployment administrators may provide a firm-specific policy outside this reusable skill. A safe policy should identify:

- Authorized calendars and naming conventions.
- Default reminder rules.
- Holiday source and verification process.
- Internal review steps before calendar writes.
- Deadline categories that require attorney approval.
- Whether calendar descriptions may include confidential facts.

Do not embed private firm names, internal URLs, passwords, calendar IDs, client facts, or account-specific instructions in this reusable skill package.
