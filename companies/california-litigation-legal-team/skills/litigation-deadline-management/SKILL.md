# Litigation Deadline Management

## Business Purpose

Convert verified litigation events into reliable proposed deadlines and clear attorney action.

## Use When

- A filing, service, hearing, order, discovery event, or agreement may create or change a deadline.
- The legal team needs a deadline calculation, reminder plan, or review of an existing date.
- Docket or correspondence monitoring reports a material scheduling event.

## Required Inputs

- Verified trigger fact and date.
- Governing rule, order, stipulation, or approved firm policy.
- Court and jurisdiction, time zone, and relevant service method.
- Requested reminders and responsible owner.

## Professional Work

1. Confirm the trigger fact and supporting source.
2. Identify the governing authority and each fact that affects calculation.
3. Calculate the proposed date and account for applicable weekends, holidays, service extensions, and court-specific rules.
4. Identify only uncertainties that could change the date.
5. Recommend reminders and responsibility appropriate to the risk.
6. After an approved calendar write, verify the resulting entry against the approved proposal.

## Attorney Deliverable

Use `references/calendar-output-format.md` to state:

- Event and proposed deadline.
- Trigger fact and governing authority.
- Material assumptions or uncertainty.
- Practical consequence and recommended action.
- Responsible owner and requested calendar action.

## Completion Standard

Analysis is complete when every proposed date has a verified trigger, governing authority, transparent calculation, and identified owner. A no-change monitor ends without a lawyer-facing report.

## Material Decisions

Calculating and proposing a date may proceed within Matter authority. Writing, inviting, notifying, or sending through a calendar requires an attorney decision. Read-back verification after an approved write does not require a second decision.

## Handoff

Return proposed dates to the Matter owner and send approved entries to the responsible calendar. Keep calculation worksheets and entry identifiers in operational records unless they affect reliability.
