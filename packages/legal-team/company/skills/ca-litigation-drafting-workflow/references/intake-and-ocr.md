# Intake And OCR

Use this reference when the drafting task depends on PDFs, scans, exhibits, pleadings, correspondence, orders, transcripts, or prior drafts.

- Confirm the Matter Safety Contract before opening source materials.
- Inspect only approved read-only source roots.
- Preserve source files exactly as received; do not split, merge, rename, reorder, or overwrite PDFs.
- Decide whether OCR is needed before relying on scanned or image-heavy PDFs.
- Treat OCR as sidecar work product under `{output_root}`, not as a replacement for the original.
- Use page-stable markers such as `--- Page 1 ---` when creating text sidecars.
- Record extraction limits, blank pages, low-confidence pages, handwritten content, rotated pages, or missing attachments.
- If a pleading has paragraph numbers, preserve paragraph references in summaries and draft support tables.
- If extraction quality is too poor for a factual assertion, log the needed input instead of drafting around it.

Safe outputs include OCR sidecars, source manifests, extraction notes, and needed-input lists under `{output_root}`.
