# MTC Workspace Setup

Use runtime paths from the Matter Safety Contract.

Recommended folders:

- `{matter_root}`: selected matter folder.
- `{output_root}`: approved intermediary work folder.
- `{output_root}/OCR`: OCR sidecars.
- `{output_root}/Tables`: source, exhibit, objection, authority, replacement, and TOC/TOA tables.
- `{output_root}/Draft Sections`: draft motion, separate statement, declarations, RJN, and proposed order sections.
- `{output_root}/QA`: final review notes and unresolved-input lists.

Rules:

- Treat `Exhibits`, `Context`, `Authorities`, `Examples`, and prior drafts as read-only unless the task says otherwise.
- Put new work product under `{output_root}`.
- Use new working copies for assembled Word drafts.
- Do not overwrite, rename, split, merge, or delete source evidence.
- Do not inspect another matter to borrow facts, templates, or authorities.
