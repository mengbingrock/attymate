# Live Draft Change Log

Maintain a change log whenever sections are confirmed, assembled, or inserted into a working draft.

Suggested columns:

- Timestamp.
- Task or delegated task.
- Artifact or Word file.
- Section changed.
- Source basis.
- Authority basis.
- Reviewer instruction or approval.
- Remaining placeholders.
- Formatting or numbering risks.
- Next owner.

Do not use the change log to store confidential facts for reuse outside the matter. Keep it matter-local under `{output_root}`.
